About the Leaf icon

The leaf pixel art icon is a simple leaf silhouette with vein detail, drawn on a 24x24 grid and one of the most versatile nature icons in the set. It signals eco-friendliness in product labels, plant health in a gardening app, natural ingredients in food or wellness contexts, and seasonal change in content themes. As a standalone it is broad enough to serve as a 'nature' category icon. Leaf-off shows a fallen leaf; leaf-plus suggests enabling a green feature. For a full tree or forest, tree icons carry a stronger sense of scale.

Usage

<!-- HTML -->
<img src="pixelarticons/svg/leaf.svg" width="24" height="24" alt="Leaf" />

<!-- React -->
import { Leaf } from 'pixelarticons/react'

<!-- Sizes: 24px, 48px, 72px, 96px (multiples of 24) -->
<img src="leaf.svg" width="48" height="48" style="image-rendering: pixelated" />
